About the tutorial

One field map, from neural forecasting to large-model reasoning.

Time-series data is fundamental to prediction and decision-making, but real-world temporal signals increasingly arrive with complementary context such as text, images, and event logs. This richer setting creates new opportunities—and new problems in alignment, heterogeneity, noise, scalability, and evaluation.

This tutorial gives a structured view of recent multi-modal time-series learning. We move from problem formulation and fusion mechanisms to pretrained language models, question answering, and foundation models, with two guided hands-on sessions designed to be runnable in Google Colab.

01

Problem & challenges

Tasks, temporal alignment, missing/noisy data, modality heterogeneity, benchmarks and evaluation.

02

Multi-modal forecasting

Sequence backbones plus late, auxiliary-variable and frequency-domain fusion strategies.

03

Reasoning & QA

Adapting pretrained language and vision-language models for temporal reasoning and natural-language interaction.

04

Foundation models

Numerical TSFMs and multimodal foundation models that integrate time series and text at scale.

Learning outcomes

What you should leave with

✓

Formalize multi-modal time-series tasks and explain the core challenges in alignment, heterogeneity, scalability and evaluation.

✓

Select appropriate forecasting strategies, including late, auxiliary-variable and frequency-domain fusion with modern sequence backbones.

✓

Understand foundation-model integration across temporal and textual modalities, including the trade-offs of numerical and multimodal pretraining.

✓

Reuse the notebooks as starting points for your own datasets and research questions.
