About the tutorial

Why multi-modal time series?

Time-series data increasingly comes with rich context—text, images, event logs, reports, and metadata. These extra modalities can improve prediction and support richer forms of temporal reasoning, but they also introduce new problems in temporal alignment, heterogeneous representations, missing information, evaluation, and scale.

This tutorial develops a field-level view of modern multi-modal time-series learning. We connect deep forecasting architectures, language-model adaptation, and time-series foundation models, with practical examples in forecasting, reasoning, and question answering.
